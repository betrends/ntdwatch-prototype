What is a Case Report?
A case report is an official record of a person presenting with signs or symptoms of a Neglected Tropical Disease (NTD). As a Community Health Worker (CHW), you are the first point of contact and your report triggers the entire care pathway for the patient.
When to Report
You should submit a report whenever you observe or are informed of a person with suspected NTD symptoms. You do not need a confirmed diagnosis — suspected cases are just as important. Timely reporting enables early intervention and prevents complications.
How to Complete Each Section
Person Information: Record the person's approximate age, sex, and precise location. A name is optional for privacy.
Condition: Select the symptom or disease that most closely matches what you observe. You may select more than one.
Social & Mental: These fields help the response team understand the person's context. Answer as accurately as possible.
